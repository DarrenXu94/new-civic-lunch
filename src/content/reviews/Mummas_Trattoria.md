Ciao! 

This review is a 2 part extravaganza. Usually my reviews are based off 1 visit but due to strong recommendations and a poor first showing I decided to give this place another go. 

First visit I got the chilli prawn linguine for $25. Quite pricey, lacking in flavour and took about 40 mins to come out. 

Second visit I got the creamy pork penne. $24 which also isn’t mega cheap, very tasty and came out rather quick. 

The general vibe is quite authentic Italian. They also had foccacia sandwiches which looked very yum for only $12. 

My general recommendation is if you pop your head in and see more than 10 ppl, come back again another day. If you get a pasta get a creamy one with cheese and pepper. 

Rating: B+
