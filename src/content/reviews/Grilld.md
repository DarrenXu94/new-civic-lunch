Yes I know I know. Everyone knows what grilld fkn is. But I am here to specifically review the lunchtime vibes of Civic Grilld.

Honestly I only came here cause I have a 2 for 1 burger coupon. I don’t really like their burgers that much. This week I came here twice and tried 3 different burgers. Wagyu, chicken and impossible meat burger. 

The individual burger rankings:

Impossible burger my fave. The meat wasn’t mega oily, and yummo flavors.

Wagyu kinda mid. It was ok but expensive ($18.50) on it’s own. Didn’t even let me sue the 2 for 1 on it.

Chicken burger was meh. Bland and wet ass lettuce.

Rating: B
