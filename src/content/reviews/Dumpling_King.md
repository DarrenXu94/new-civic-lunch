As a Chinese boi I have very high standards for dumps. Normally I wouldn’t even get them when I eat out but this place is named after the lil nugget so I had to try them. My verdict? Meh

12 pieces for $13. I also got the lamb with cumin $19 to share. 12 pieces was defs not enough. Taste was standard, nothing special.

Vibes inside a bit dead too. At least it had plenty of seating cause not many people were around.

Rating: B
