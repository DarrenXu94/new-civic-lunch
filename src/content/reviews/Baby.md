Previously known as Baby Su

Really surprised me. I used to shit on this place all the time for white ppl wanted to try a bit of ethnic. I mean it still is not ethnic at all but it was actually hella tasty.

I got the Soba noodle bowl with terriyaki chicken for $23. Surprising amount of chicken and was actually cooked very nice. Great seating area inside with good vibes. Generally a pleasant experience.

Rating: A
