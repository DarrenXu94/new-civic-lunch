Gaon is a cute lil korean restaurant with $17 lunch deals. What could go wrong? Well unfortunately quite a lot. 

I got the bibimpbap in a soup and not only did it take forever to come out, it was so hot that I had to wait ages for it to cool down before I could eat it. By that time I had already burnt my tongue and couldn't taste anything. That turned a 30 min quick lunch into a 1 hr wait.

I cannot comment on the flavours either as I couldn’t taste any of them. Nice sides though (these came out before the tongue crispening).

Rating: B-
