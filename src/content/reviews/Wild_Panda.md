Wild panda is a cute lil Chinese restaurant specialising in spicy food. However today was not the day for spicy cause I was wearing a white t shirt and it was 34 degrees outside. Having said that, they have heaps of non spicy options and I got a plate of pork dumps for $19. 

The cooking time seemed a bit long for what I assume is dropping pre-made dumps into hot water (15 to 20 mins) and there were only like 3 other ppl in the restaurant. They were also playing this weird playlist of soft spoken acoustic covers (one song was Creep from Radiohead) so I was not vibing too hard with that.

The food however tasted pretty yummy. Had some garnishes and came with dipping sauce.

Price is a bit steep but otherside pleasant enough time.

Rating: B
